# Third-party data and sound attribution

## Grand piano

Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5, by **Alexander Holm**, licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ .

Seven C-octave MP3 recordings are redistributed locally from https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander . Original author README is retained in `public/audio/piano/README`. The app pitch-shifts these recordings and changes playback envelopes; it does not redistribute a full multi-velocity instrument. Source recordings remain unchanged.

## WordNet

Princeton WordNet 3.1 database through `wordnet-db`. WordNet licensing and attribution are retained in the installed dataset (LICENSE/LICENSE.txt as distributed). Princeton University owns the WordNet database; its permission notice applies to data redistribution. Definitions/examples are dataset entries, not generated definitions. WordNet content is not a substitute for legal or cultural review.

## CMU

CMU Pronouncing Dictionary through `cmu-pronouncing-dictionary` (ISC package and included source notices). See the installed package license and https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict for data provenance. Pronunciation coverage is primarily American English.
