# SIRIUS design system

## Supplied references

`SRS-BNDL.pdf`: violet-lit stage imagery, deep black headings/headers, white/silver typography, lilac gradients, fine violet dividers and outlined panels.
`SRSPRNT.pdf`: intact SIRIUS badge, bold display headings, black/white surfaces, purple stage lighting and a narrow violet horizon line.
The chat logo reinforces the silver badge, black field and electric-violet surround. Documents are visual references; their event inventory and pricing are not app requirements.

## Logo

`public/brand/sirius-entertainment.png` is extracted from the logo region on page 1 of the supplied SRSPRNT.pdf. It preserves the artwork and surrounding dark field, without recoloring, redrawing, stretching, removing the background, or adding filters. The file is 580×360 pixels including padding; its source is raster artwork. The high-resolution chat image was visible in conversation but was not exposed as a downloadable file, so the PDF artwork is the available source. Replace with a standalone original if higher resolution is supplied; no recreated logo is used.

Shared Brand component appears in welcome and desktop navigation. Product name is separate typography. Small mobile navigation prioritizes controls; the official badge appears on mobile welcome.

## Tokens and use

- Background #09070f; panels #13101c; raised #21192e; border #30263e.
- Text/silver #f5f3fa; muted #b2aabd; readable dark-mode accent #d6a9ff.
- Violet #a600f2 and deep violet #6200b8 are visual-reference-derived UI choices, not claimed official spot-color specifications.
- Primary buttons use violet gradients with white text. Fine violet dividers and subdued radial lighting echo the documents.
- Bold system sans-serif headings reflect the supplied print designs; serif lyric examples retain a creative writing feel. No claim to use an official brand font.
- Light mode uses pale lilac surfaces and deep-violet text; logo artwork remains unchanged.
- Keep writing areas calm: reserve lighting effects for welcome/dashboard; no flashing, moving beams or visual noise.
- Visible focus, reduced motion, responsive navigation, explicit field labels, and contrast-checked primary text/button combinations.
